## 6. Mandatories & no-gos (verbatim from the brief)

**Mandatories**
- The product name Meltemi Fizz is always written in Latin script, in full — never transliterated, never abbreviated to "Fizz".
- Logo clear space: minimum 1× the height of the wordmark on all sides.
- Primary palette: Aegean Blue #1B4F8A, Citrus Yellow #F5C518. Backgrounds must not use gradients over photography.
- Permitted claims: "zero sugar", "φυσικά συστατικά" (per approved ingredient list).
- Brand guidelines version 3.1 is the applicable version.
- Strategy is TikTok-first: the entire creative approach must be designed primarily for TikTok.

**No-gos**
- Not permitted: any health or weight-loss claim; any comparison naming a competitor brand.
- No alcohol association of any kind (imagery, occasions, co-branding).
- Avoid generic "beach party" clichés; the brand leans premium-everyday, not spring break.
