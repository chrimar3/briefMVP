Creative Brief (draft) — Meltemi Fizz launch

1Single-minded proposition — the one thing the campaign must say

Meltemi Fizz is the Greek-made sparkling tea that refreshes your everyday — zero sugar, nothing to feel guilty about.

A candidate, not a client line: the brief records no agreed proposition. [key_messages; open_questions/key_messages]
