What you get

  • The client brief in Greek and in English, every line carrying its citation, with a readiness verdict computed by code.

  • Every contradiction seeded in this project, both sides quoted, waiting for a decision.

What code refuses to let through

  • Every cited anchor in the brief object exists word for word in its source (the documents may quote in translation); a failing output is sent back once, then the run stops.
  • Channel specs come from the traffic team’s table, never from a model.
  • Nothing passes the sign-off gate without a person; a model cannot write that field.

Who touches it, where the data goes
