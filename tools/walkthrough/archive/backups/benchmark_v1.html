A brief with receiptsΈνα brief με αποδείξεις

Brief Builder turns a client’s pile of documents into a Greek and English brief in which every claim shows its source, disagreements sit side by side, gaps become questions, and a person signs before anything moves. This is one complete run, sheet by sheet.

How to read the marks

  • Pencil drafts. AI reads the documents and drafts. Its stamps are hollow: a draft is provisional by construction.
  • Rule checks. Plain code checks every draft against the sources. Its work sits on ruled ledger stripes; a failed check is a black cross, never red.
  • Red pen decides. Only a person writes in red. No machine sheet carries it; it arrives at the sign-off and is carried forward from there.

Press Next or the → key to turn the sheet; ← goes back; C opens the contents; long sheets scroll as usual. Each sheet shows what went in, what came out and who was responsible. The ledger at the foot of a sheet holds the technical record for whoever wants it.

What it is

A two-stage pipeline. Stage one reads the client’s documents and drafts the brief with receipts; an account lead signs. Stage two, only after sign-off, drafts a creative brief in shadow mode for a creative lead to judge.

  • Resolve a contradiction: both sides are shown and a person chooses.
  • Correct what a person said: garbles are flagged, never rewritten.

1Single-minded proposition

Meltemi Fizz is the Greek-made sparkling tea that refreshes your everyday — zero sugar, nothing to feel guilty about.

Built from the zero-sugar, no-guilt angle and the ownership positioning [key_messages]. The brief records no client-agreed proposition [open_questions/key_messages]; this line is a candidate for the creative lead to weigh, not a client line. “Greek-made” is the model’s upgrade of the brief’s “Greek brand” positioning: a manufacturing-origin claim no source supports and the guidelines do not list, for the creative lead to strike or verify.

2Core insight

The 25–40 urban professional has quietly outgrown the sugary soft drink, but doesn’t want to be lectured about health to feel good about a daily refreshment. A drink they reach for on an ordinary afternoon is a small act of self-respect — modern, unfussy, and theirs.

Audience per the resolved conflict [conflicts/audiences · resolved_by_human]. The “no lecturing” truth rests on the mandatory that bars health claims and the premium-everyday steer [mandatories].

No-gos
