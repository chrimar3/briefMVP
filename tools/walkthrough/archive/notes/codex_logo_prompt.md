You are the design lead at a small studio. Design THREE distinct logo/lockup directions for "Meltemi Fizz", a zero-sugar sparkling tea from Meltemi Beverages (a synthetic demo brand; everything is a walkthrough illustration, never a deliverable). Read fixtures/northlight_01/background_brand_guidelines.md first: its rules are hard constraints. Then look at the current lockup at tools/walkthrough/kv_previous_logo_ref.png and its application on a can at tools/walkthrough/kv_can_A_previous_logo.png: the client's verdict on it is "generic, not ownable: ring-plus-leaf could be any wellness drink". Your job is a mark only Meltemi Fizz can own.

TERRITORY (decided by the client): the wind itself. The meltemi is the dry north wind of the Aegean summer; the name is the brand's biggest asset. Each direction must make the wind the mark in a single gesture, ideally one that also reads as rising fizz, so name, category and "premium summer" arrive together. Three DISTINCT ideas within that territory, for example (starting points, not mandates): the wind drawn as one continuous stroke that resolves into rising bubbles; the zero-sugar zero blown open, a ring drawn in one wind stroke with a tail; a reinterpreted meteorological wind glyph or pennant. Avoid: waves, sun, beach, palm, sailboats, spring-break energy, a slim can, any alcohol cue, a leaf-in-a-ring (that is the rejected mark). Tone: premium-everyday, confident modern Greek, quiet humour allowed.

HARD CONSTRAINTS (from the guidelines and the page's deterministic checks):
- Exactly three flat colours: Aegean Blue #1B4F8A, Citrus Yellow #F5C518, white #FFFFFF. Flat fills only: no gradients, no opacity or stroke-opacity attributes, no filters, no masks, no images. Only hex colours in fill/stroke attributes.
- The product name "Meltemi Fizz" always in Latin script, in full, never abbreviated, never transliterated. Logo clear space at least 1× the wordmark height on all sides: nothing drawn (and no circle edge) inside y 1440–1772 across x 825–1735, except the wordmark itself.
- Each direction is ONE standalone SVG file with this exact frame so the page can use it as it uses the current art:
  <svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 2560 1920"> … </svg>, first element a full-frame rect fill="#1B4F8A" (or, if a direction wants a white field, a white rect; say so).
  The wordmark element must be exactly: <text x="1280" y="1660" text-anchor="middle" font-family="'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, Arial, 'Liberation Sans', sans-serif" font-weight="700" font-size="112" letter-spacing="-3" fill="#FFFFFF">Meltemi Fizz</text> (you may change fill to #F5C518 or #1B4F8A on a white field, and letter-spacing, nothing else; if the direction wants custom lettering, draw it as paths ABOVE this text and keep this text element too, it is what the checks read).
  The line element must be exactly: <text x="1280" y="1840" text-anchor="middle" font-family="'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, Arial, 'Liberation Sans', sans-serif" font-weight="700" font-size="44" letter-spacing="6" fill="#F5C518">SPARKLING TEA · ZERO SUGAR</text> (use the literal middle dot character or &#183;, never &middot;: XML has no HTML entities and the render breaks).
