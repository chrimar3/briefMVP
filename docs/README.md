# Documents — reading order and status

Every document in the repository that a reader might open, with its status on 2026-09-23.

- **Current**: describes the system as it is now; kept up to date.
- **Frozen**: a specification that is read-only by rule; later owner decisions may supersede parts of it.
- **Historical**: a record of what was done or measured at the time; kept unchanged, never edited to look current.
- **Superseded**: replaced; the replacement is named.

Where any document disagrees with [`OPERATING_DECISIONS.md`](OPERATING_DECISIONS.md), the decision
record prevails. Measured figures carry their routing era: **Haiku-era** means the July 2026
routing (extraction on Haiku 4.5); the **current routing** (since 2026-07-30: extraction on Sonnet
plus an independent `verify-extract` reader per source) has not yet been measured for a whole brief.

## Known discrepancies in historical records

Historical records are not edited; these notes say how to read them.

- **Agency size.** The PRD's case is a ~40-person agency (`docs/PRD.md`, header).
  `runs/tier_7_report.md` and the Tier 5 and Tier 7 plans in `superpowers/plans/` say "a
  50-person agency". The PRD figure is the reference.
- **Harness history.** `eval/harness.py` has two commits: the Tier-1 freeze (`9e371f6`) and a
  repo-relative path fallback (`7320689`, 2026-07-29; path plumbing only, no acceptance criterion
  touched). Earlier text that says the file has one commit predates or overlooks the second.
- **Tier-3 dollars.** Earlier reports quote $4.29 for the graded run; that total counts each
  creative arm twice. The de-duplicated spend is $3.55 ($2.81 for Stage 1). `EVIDENCE.md` shows both.
- **Returned hours.** PRD §10 and older cost text say "~€5–6k/yr"; the PRD's own assumptions give
  (120 − 50) min × 180 briefs ÷ 60 = 210 h × €19–20 = €3 990–4 200.
- **Creative scope.** The PRD, Tier 0–4 reports and the July documents describe creative as
  shadow-only ("never delivered"). Superseded by `OPERATING_DECISIONS.md` (2026-09-20, and the
  pilot scope of 2026-09-22).
- **AGENTS.md.** First committed in Tier 6 (`5c386ba`) as a mechanical copy of `CLAUDE.md` that
  named "Codex subagents" and `.Codex/agents/`; rewritten on 2026-09-23 as a pointer.
- **Uncommitted runs.** Some documents cite local runs that are gitignored and not in the
  repository: `runs/routing-validate-01` (the one current-routing extraction leg),
  `runs/tier3-confirm` and `runs/tier3-confirm2` (the $2.07–2.44 cost range),
  `runs/evidence-20260729` (the continuous 29 July capture). Their figures are quoted, labelled as
  local, and cannot be re-derived from a clone.
- **docs/DECK.md** is cited by some tier reports and plans but is deliberately untracked (owner's
  decision); it is not part of the repository.
